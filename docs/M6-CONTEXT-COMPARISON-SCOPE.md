# Milestone 6 — Issuer context versus the opening gap

This is a scope proposal for an offline development comparison. The [v2 feasibility check](../reports/m6-historical-opening-result-2026-11-15.json) found four disagreements in 107 verified openings and closed that design. The new question is whether an issuer's earlier earnings reactions improve an estimate of the remaining regular-session move after accounting for the opening gap.

## What the existing data can support

The coverage check verified the frozen Milestone 4 inputs and used only its 128-event development dataset, which includes the old Milestone 4 test block. Cluster deduplication retained all 128 events, spanning 23 issuers and 73 reaction sessions. Both the opening and Day-1 close labels were present and uncaveated on 119 rows. ## Proposed development comparison

- **Common clock and target.** Freeze issuer context at the event's recorded pre-open news cutoff. Both models receive the same opening return `g = O / Cprev − 1` at the opening reference. Let `y = C / Cprev − 1` be the Day-1 close return. The response is the sign-adjusted fade return `z = −sign(g) × ((1 + y) / (1 + g) − 1)`. Exclude zero gaps from both models and report their count. Use only a consistent prior-close and share basis. In development the committed daily open is a proxy for the opening observation. Any future study would require comparable validated official opens. A daily bar supplies no opening receipt timestamp.
- **Opening-gap baseline.** Fit one fixed ridge regression of `z` on absolute gap and gap sign, so both models can account for different behavior after up and down opens. Include an intercept and standardize on training data only. Use the established Milestone 4 ridge convention of penalty 1 on coefficients and an unpenalized intercept.
- **Issuer-context challenger.** Add exactly one feature. At each row's news cutoff, compute `(sum of prior own-issuer close returns + 10 × pooled mean) / (n + 10)` using only earlier, mature, clean training labels. Use the difference from that same pooled mean, expressed in signed residual-return units as `-sign(g) × (shrunk estimate − pooled mean) / (1 + g)`. The opening observation translates the two forecasts to the same units. It cannot enter the pre-open history calculation. A training row with no mature issuer history gets zero issuer deviation. A test row with no own history uses the baseline forecast and is counted as a fallback. Log the supporting event IDs and maturity times. Give both arms identical training and test row eligibility. Preserve the issuer-history estimate separately from the final opening-clock forecast.
- **Validation and score.** Fit on blocks 1–2, 1–3, and 1–4 and score once on blocks 3, 4, and 5 respectively. Keep reaction sessions and economic clusters whole. Apply a strict cutoff to all fit targets and to the history for each row. The primary score is paired out-of-fold squared-error improvement, baseline loss minus challenger loss, on every eligible test row. Use clean-window as primary and all-event as a sensitivity. Report fold, issuer, session, sign, fallback and attrition counts, relative loss improvement and a 99% interval envelope from resampling issuers and reaction sessions separately. The continuous score can distinguish forecast quality even when two flag rules agree. Coverage-matched flag analysis, if later requested, is a separate design decision.
- **Advance or stop.** Proposed development floors are 40 paired clean test rows, at least 10 for each opening-gap sign, and at least 15 issuers and 20 sessions, with at least two test folds contributing 10 paired rows each. For further study planning, require the lower endpoint of the 99% envelope to exceed zero, a point estimate of at least 5% relative MSE reduction against a positive baseline MSE, and a nonnegative all-event point estimate. These are proposed gates for further work. The existing sample supplies no assurance of power. A replay on these development blocks cannot establish a new out-of-sample edge.

## Recommendation

Authorize a narrow pre-registration and one offline development replay if the owner wants to test issuer history. Freeze the exact rule, seeds and gates before scoring. Stop if support or improvement falls short. A favorable development result would justify scoping a new untouched historical cohort, confirming matched official opening coverage and basis, and planning sample size using development-only paired-loss dispersion. It would still establish no live receipt latency. The 43-event Milestone 5 extension and any prior feasibility sample are excluded from fresh confirmation. The proposed 2027 study stays on hold.

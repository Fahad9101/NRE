# Milestone 6 annual guidance comparison registration

The registered experiment asks whether changes in annual consolidated revenue guidance improve forecasts beyond opening-gap size and direction. It uses the 58 frozen held events whose raw official openings were verified across 20 issuers and 46 sessions. The three held blocks contain 27, 18 and 13 events. Raw first-minute coverage passed in GitHub Actions run 44102104068 on commit `4f0b3eb96e7bb645900794f6966c48a6888fd7ab`. [Raw verification record](../reports/m6-guidance-expanded-raw-openings-2026-11-19.json) · [Workflow run](https://github.com/Fahad9101/NRE/actions/runs/44102104068)

The baseline fits a ridge regression of the sign-adjusted opening-to-close fade on absolute opening gap and gap sign. The challenger adds one fixed column, the inverse hyperbolic sine of the guidance midpoint revision multiplied by the negative gap sign. The sign aligns a raised revenue forecast with the response after either opening direction. Both arms use identical rows, training-only scaling, the established ridge penalty of one and an unpenalized intercept. [Registered protocol](../config/m6-guidance-protocol.json)

Training starts with January through June 2025 and expands only through earlier held blocks. Every fit close must mature strictly before the first feature cutoff of the held block. Each fold requires at least 20 common fit rows across 10 issuers. This is a separate engineering choice for the four-parameter guidance model. The fixed warmup appears to offer approximately 24 potential comparable updates before quality checks, so the earlier revenue study's 30-row default would exceed current support. If a fold fails the registered training minimum, the comparison stops with insufficient support. [Frozen cohort and dates](../config/m6-guidance-frame.json)

Source quality is determined before closing labels are acquired. Original timestamped disclosures establish comparability, the active prior guidance and the observation window. Material transactions, chief executive succession, workforce changes, litigation and unresolved price-basis changes are hard exclusions. Routine buyback and dividend announcements and minor appointments are the fixed soft caveats masked in the clean primary. The sensitivity retains otherwise valid rows with those caveats. Each source mask must be committed before market outcomes, using the same rule for warmup and held events. [Quality rules](../config/m6-guidance-protocol.json)

The pooled primary score is the mean paired squared-error improvement over the baseline. Advancing to fresh-study scoping requires at least 40 paired rows across 15 issuers and 20 sessions, 10 up gaps and 10 down gaps, and two folds with at least 10 pairs. It also requires at least 5% relative MSE improvement, a positive lower bound of the issuer/session 99% bootstrap envelope, and a nonnegative sensitivity improvement. Five thousand resamples and seeds 640001 and 640002 are fixed. Four guidance cuts span only two issuers, limiting directional inference. The development selection and reconstructed availability clocks also limit interpretation. [Decision rules](../config/m6-guidance-protocol.json)

The next implementation step is to lock all warmup features and source dispositions, verify their raw opens, then acquire common-basis price labels under the registered rules. Label commitments and acquisition quality must be frozen before one logged scoring attempt. This registration has performed zero model runs. The proposed 2027 study remains on hold.

# Owner-approved NRE research closeout

The owner approved closing the current NRE research track, retaining its research assets and stopping further development on November 23, 2026. The exact approval was: **“okay do the three recommendations”**. It referred to the three recommendations recorded below. [Owner decision record](../reports/nre-research-closeout-2026-11-23.json)

1. **Close the research track with a negative finding.** The tested approaches did not demonstrate the required predictive advantage. The single registered annual-guidance comparison failed all three performance gates: prediction MSE increased 2.8845% on 51 clean held events, its outer 99% uncertainty envelope crossed zero, and sensitivity MSE increased 0.7922%. This finding is specific to the tested approach, sample and comparator. [Final result](M6-GUIDANCE-RESULT.md)
2. **Retain the repository and research assets.** Preserve the existing audited event definitions, source evidence, code, tests, reports, historical archive and Git history. Published commit `89e10d9f40c793489a30a1cea0d51cda728d0c4f` anchors the completed research before this status update. Existing hash-only commitments and frozen ledgers remain intact. [Result](../reports/m6-guidance-result-2026-11-23.json) · [Run provenance](../reports/m6-guidance-result-run-2026-11-23.json) · [Immutable attempt](../reports/m6-guidance-attempt-v2-001.json)
3. **Stop further development under the current approach.** Defer the M6 Reaction Gap Engine, fresh validation, M7 real-time ranking, M8 paper trading and M9 integration. Keep the proposed 2027 study on hold. The accepted status of Milestones 1–5 is unchanged. Production milestones remain incomplete; this closeout records the research decision rather than production delivery.

The earlier November 15 M6 research acknowledgment, all frozen protocols, source masks, acquisition commitments, attempts and results remain historical evidence. The agreed single comparison has been consumed and will not be tuned or rerun after its outcome. [Earlier acknowledgment](../reports/m6-research-closeout-acknowledgment-2026-11-15.json) · [Registered protocol](../config/m6-guidance-protocol-v2.json)

The repository remains accessible for audit, reproduction and reuse. Routine integrity checks and synthetic tests can continue; they do not reopen research or authorize another empirical comparison. GitHub archival settings, workflows and secrets are unchanged by this closeout. For the final guidance comparison, raw provider values were retained only privately during execution; its versioned evidence retains commitments rather than raw event prices or returns.

Any future NRE research requires a separate owner decision to reopen the work, a materially different hypothesis, an untouched cohort and predefined success criteria. There is no further implementation work under the closed approach.

The broader roster contains 100 issuers from the September 30, 2024 Invesco QQQ Trust holdings filed before the study window. A fixed 17-issuer software and IT services pilot yielded 11 qualification and guidance pairs. ## Frame and pilot

The roster preserves 101 securities, including Alphabet's two share classes. The official N-PORT filing supplies issuer names and security identifiers. Its SEC-rendered representation was retrieved through NRE's public relay and pinned. The audited holdings supply the software and IT services classifications for the pilot. The frame fixes a calendar 2024 qualification window and a January 2, 2025 through September 30, 2026 retrospective development window. It excludes previously studied issuer CIKs from the two pinned NRE cohorts once identity is resolved. [Locked frame](../config/m6-guidance-frame.json) · [Audited holdings](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000119312524285734/d876223dn30b2.htm)

Issuer qualification requires two dated 2024 earnings releases with directly stated consolidated USD revenue guidance for the same fiscal year. The 11 candidates have a subsequent same-year pair of ranges dated before their 2025 feature cutoffs, using original wire or SEC filing clocks. Eight raised the revenue midpoint and three reaffirmed it. Six issuers lacked a qualifying pair in the source review. The pilot tested one readily verifiable event per qualified issuer. It leaves intervening-disclosure and accounting-basis checks for full eligibility, including review of Palo Alto Networks' pending acquisition at its May update. ## Opening evidence and next gate

The opening probe queried only the first minute of each matched session. The connected Alpaca tool returned errors during parallel calls. Sequential requests recovered access. ## Expanded issuer screen

The remaining 83 roster issuers all have a recorded disposition. One CIK already belongs to an earlier NRE cohort and was excluded. The other 82 were checked against public SEC submissions metadata. Of 324 original Item 2.02 candidates for the 2024 screen, 318 had readable exhibits. Eleven additional issuers had two verified same-fiscal-year guidance disclosures. Twenty supplied no matching annual range in the reviewed source material, six had no Item 2.02 filing in the retrieved window, and 45 remain unconfirmed. This is a lower bound on issuer qualification. The unconfirmed issuers remain in the screening ledger. [Issuer and source evidence](../reports/m6-guidance-expanded-evidence-2026-11-17.json)

The held-period review anchored 75 guidance pairs in the original filing archives and withheld 17 for concurrent catalysts, inconsistent consolidation basis or unresolved dated listing evidence. The 58 retained candidates have dated Nasdaq cover evidence and reconstructed guidance availability before the scheduled open minus 15 minutes. The SEC-based clock adds two minutes to acceptance and respects the New York filing date. It supplies a historical availability proxy. ## Expanded opening coverage

The same-event historical SIP check found a unique Nasdaq official-open condition on all 58 candidates across 20 issuers and 46 reaction sessions. The held blocks contributed 27, 18 and 13 matches. These counts provisionally exceed the planning floors of 40 matches, 15 issuers and 20 sessions, with 10 matches in at least two held blocks. The 58 guidance pairs include 40 raises, 14 unchanged midpoints and four cuts. The cuts span two issuers, limiting any directional interpretation. [Opening coverage and event dispositions](../reports/m6-guidance-expanded-open-observations-2026-11-17.json)

The expanded raw workflow verified all 58 official opens in 70 requests, preserving the 20-issuer, 46-session support and all three held-block counts. The comparison protocol is now registered against that exact verified membership. Earlier fit rows, source-quality masks and common opening-gap and close label commitments are the remaining preparation steps before scoring. The coverage audit requested zero reaction-session closing outcomes. The 2027 study remains on hold.

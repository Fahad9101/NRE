## Frame and pilot

The roster preserves 101 securities, including Alphabet's two share classes. The official N-PORT filing supplies issuer names and security identifiers. Its SEC-rendered representation was retrieved through NRE's public relay and pinned. The audited holdings supply the software and IT services classifications for the pilot. The frame fixes a calendar 2024 qualification window and a January 2, 2025 through September 30, 2026 retrospective development window. It excludes previously studied issuer CIKs from the two pinned NRE cohorts once identity is resolved. [Locked frame](../config/m6-guidance-frame.json) · [Audited holdings](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000119312524285734/d876223dn30b2.htm)

Issuer qualification requires two dated 2024 earnings releases with directly stated consolidated USD revenue guidance for the same fiscal year. The 11 candidates have a subsequent same-year pair of ranges dated before their 2025 feature cutoffs, using original wire or SEC filing clocks. Eight raised the revenue midpoint and three reaffirmed it. Six issuers lacked a qualifying pair in the source review. The pilot tested one readily verifiable event per qualified issuer. It leaves intervening-disclosure and accounting-basis checks for full eligibility, including review of Palo Alto Networks' pending acquisition at its May update. ## Opening evidence and next gate

The opening probe queried only the first minute of each matched session. The connected Alpaca tool returned errors during parallel calls. Sequential requests recovered access. Splitting busy intervals and increasing the response limit resolved the saturated results. Each completed window contained one unique condition `Q` on Nasdaq venue `Q` and tape `C`. The connected tool formats trade records without the underlying correction fields. The repository now has a manual workflow that uses the existing historical SIP connection and the pinned pilot to check raw correction markers and conflicting prints. It reports only counts and event dispositions. Run that workflow on the 11 frozen events, then qualify the remaining 83 issuers from pre-window disclosures. Enumerate quarterly releases and reconcile the latest effective prior guidance before checking opening coverage. Calculate held coverage by reaction session, issuer and chronological block. The development planning floors are 40 eligible matches across 15 issuers and 20 sessions, with at least 10 pairs in each of two held blocks. The broader sample also needs cuts as well as raises. The pilot contains only April–June 2025 events and establishes neither held-sample support nor performance. The coverage check requested zero reaction-session closing outcomes. The 2027 study remains on hold.
